## 3. Logo Brief
- **Idea:** "The Lens of Profit." A stylized camera aperture that doubles as a growth chart (upward arrow) or a coin slot.
- **Mark Type:** Symbol + Wordmark.
- **Visual Goal:** Must feel like a FinTech app, not a toy. Sharp edges, geometric precision.
- **Requirements:** 16px favicon, 1024px App Store icon (No transparency).

## 4. Voice Guide
**Core Identity:** Authoritative, Efficient, and Results-Driven.

- **Authoritative**, not arrogant. (We know what works, but we're your partner).
- **Efficient**, not abrupt. (Save time, but stay professional).
- **Results-Driven**, not hypey. (ROI over "magic").

### Do's and Don'ts
| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Maximize your ROAS." | "Make magic videos!" |
| "Generate high-converting assets." | "Create cool clips." |
| "Deployment successful." | "Yay! Your video is done!" |
| "Optimized for TikTok conversion." | "Looks great on mobile." |
| "Scale your creative output." | "Make a bunch of videos." |
